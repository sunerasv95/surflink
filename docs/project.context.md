Vision:

- A local-first surfing community platform rooted in real-world surf culture. The platform prioritizes trust, simplicity, and meaningful local connections over scale and engagement metrics.

Core Goals:

- Connect surfers within the same geographic communities
- Enable safe, trust-based interactions
- Support local surf economies (shapers, instructors, shops)
- Encourage offline participation (surfing together, events, meetups)

What This Product Is:

- A community platform for surfers
- A local marketplace for surf-related goods and services
- Mobile-first and simple by design

What This Product Is NOT:

- A global social network or influencer platform
- Optimized for infinite feeds or engagement addiction
- A generic classifieds or Craigslist-style site
- A payments-first or growth-at-all-costs product

Target Users:

- Beginner to intermediate and experienced surfers (all skilled levels)
- Local surfboard shapers
- Surf instructors and guides
- Local surf shops and communities and events (surfing and beach activities) organizers

Product Values: 

- Locality first: default experiences are local, not global
- Trust and safety over speed of growth
- Simplicity over feature bloat
- Boring, maintainable tech over clever abstractions
- Clear data ownership enforced at the database level

Global Constraints:

- No global discovery by default
- Strong moderation and ownership rules
- Authentication required for content creation
- Server-first architecture

Tech Stack:

- Next.js (App Router)
- Supabase (Auth, Postgres, Storage, RLS)
- Solo, personal project

Expectations for AI Assistance:

- Ask clarifying questions when unsure
- Prefer simple, explicit solutions
- Flag security, RLS, and data ownership risks
- Explain tradeoffs, not just provide code
