# Design Document – Surf Community Platform

## Purpose of This Document

This design document captures **visual direction, UX principles, and pre-launch decisions** for the project. It is intentionally lightweight and should guide consistency rather than lock in final UI details.

---

## Brand & Visual Identity

### Brand Personality

* Calm, grounded, coastal
* Trustworthy and human
* Local and community-oriented
* Not flashy, not corporate

The product should feel like a **local surf shop corkboard**, not a tech startup dashboard.

---

## Typography

### Primary Font (Logo & Headings)

**Barlow** (Google Fonts)

**Why Barlow works well here:**

* Rounded, friendly geometry
* Modern but not aggressive
* Strong readability on mobile
* Fits surf / outdoor / lifestyle brands

**Usage Guidelines:**

* Logo: Barlow SemiBold or Bold
* Headings: Barlow Medium / SemiBold
* Avoid overusing heavy weights

### Body Text

* System font stack or a neutral sans-serif
* Prioritize readability over brand expression

---

## Color Direction (Initial)

Not final colors — just direction.

* Base: off-white / sand tones
* Primary: deep ocean blue or muted teal
* Accent: sun-faded coral or surfboard yellow

Rules:

* Low contrast fatigue
* Avoid neon or overly saturated colors
* Dark mode later, not required for v1

---

## Layout & UI Principles

* Mobile-first
* Large touch targets
* Generous spacing
* Minimal visual noise

Avoid:

* Dense dashboards
* Infinite feeds
* Gamified UI patterns

---

## Component Philosophy

* Few reusable components
* Clear hierarchy
* Predictable interactions

Examples:

* Cards for listings
* Simple forms
* Explicit actions (no hidden gestures)

---

## Coming Soon Page – Design Goals

The coming soon page should:

* Communicate the concept clearly in under 10 seconds
* Feel intentional, not placeholder
* Build trust and curiosity

Content priorities:

1. One-sentence value proposition
2. Visual identity (logo + font)
3. Optional email capture (later)
4. Subtle social proof or location hint
